AI in physics, materials and quantum engineering

Does this update your priors?

On 31 May 2025 James bet a tungsten cube that quantum computing is obvious bullshit. He said he would reconsider if he saw a single huge leap in metallurgy or other physical engineering for which AI was a necessary input. This page lists the AI results since then, each with its source, its caveat and Eric's rating against that test. Eric wins the cube if James changes his mind.

Below: the bet as it was made, three of James's sentences, then 17 results since the bet and 6 from before it.
