Eric Wall's tungsten cube #1

PlanB's November target

On 1 November 2021 PlanB posted a target of more than $98K for Bitcoin in November. Two days later Eric bet a cube that it would fail, and Adam Back took the bet 18 minutes later. Eric unveiled the cube on 28 December as piece #1 of his collection.

Won from Adam Back (@adam3us). Announced 28 Dec 2021.
