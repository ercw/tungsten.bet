Eric Wall's tungsten cube bets

tungsten.bet

Eric Wall makes public bets for a 1.5-inch tungsten cube. If he loses once, the winner gets every cube in his collection and he stops betting.

5 cubes won  ·  0 lost  ·  1 open

The rules, in his words

  1. Bets are made in public, and the stake is always one 1.5-inch cube. The rule for these bets is that all bets must be entered publicly. The bet is always for a 1.5” tungsten cube. (ercwl, 28 Dec 2021)
  2. The loser pays for the cube. To Brad Mills: The total amount payable is $438 (994 404 sats) with import taxes and shipping, plus the engraving of your face next to @ck_SNARKs as promised. (ercwl, 1 Jan 2024)
  3. One loss ends it. If I lose once, I will give that person ALL MY CUBES and stop betting. (ercwl, 26 May 2023)
  4. He only offers the bet when he is confident. I only make these bets when I have very high conviction. (ercwl, 26 May 2023)

Never lost

As long as my tungsten bet collection is growing, it means I have never lost a tungsten bet. (ercwl, 26 May 2023)

On 5 January 2025 he put the collection at five: if you win you win all my cubes. they’re 5 now (ercwl, 5 Jan 2025)

A search of his posts on X found no lost bet, and no new cube announced after January 2025. The search was not a full read of his archive.
