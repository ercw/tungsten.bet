AI in physics, materials and quantum engineering, 2022–2026

Does this update your priors?

On 31 May 2025 James bet a tungsten cube that quantum computing is obvious bullshit, and named his own test for changing his mind: a single huge leap in metallurgy or other physical engineering for which AI was a necessary input. This page puts the AI results of 2022–2026 on that ladder, rung by rung, with the author's ratings marked as such. It cannot score the bet. Only James can.
