James's test as a ladder

His test, split into steps. An item reaches a rung only if it also reached every rung below it. Pick a row to keep only the items that reach it; the others are dimmed in the list further down.

  1. rung 1, AI touched matter. AI was an input to something that was synthesized, fabricated or physically controlled. James expected these first: I think we'll see early signs well ahead in more primitive forms of engineering.
  2. rung 2, AI was necessary. The result could not have been reached the same way without the AI. James: for which AI was a necessary input.
  3. rung 3, a huge leap. James's bar: a single huge leap in metallurgy or some other kind of physical engineering.
  4. qubits. Separate from the rungs: does it bear on building many physical qubits? James: buildable and operatable by human construction for some huge number of physical qbits.
